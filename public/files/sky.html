Core Identity

Role / Class

Homebrew Creature PC — Martial Striker (Monk / Ranger base, shadow-void flavored)

Communication

Speaks Common. Tone is sparse and precise — a creature of few words and deliberate action.

Origins & Lore

Sky was not always a servant. He was, once, a hunter — a naturally born creature of predatory instinct, stalking prey across untamed lands with relentless precision. He does not remember how long he hunted. He only knows that he found something he could not kill.

Whatever he pursued, it turned on him. The confrontation was brief. Sky lost. Rather than end his life, the being he hunted offered a deal — service in exchange for survival. Sky accepted. He had no choice.

The hunter who existed before that moment is mostly gone now — eroded by time, trauma, or something stranger. What remains is a creature defined entirely by obligation: loyal not out of love or fear, but because the vow is the only architecture his fractured self still stands on. He does not know who he used to be. He knows only what he owes.

Physical Characteristics

Sky stands at roughly medium humanoid height — a striking chimeric raptor covered in vivid plumage of deep teal, burnt orange, and white. His crested head is framed by spined feathers that flare when agitated. His forelimbs end in dexterous taloned hands; his powerful hind legs carry him with the coiled, deliberate grace of a predator that has never stopped being one.

His void nature is completely concealed at rest. To any observer — magical or mundane — he registers as rare, exotic fauna. No cold aura. No shadow bleed. No anomalous readings. The darkness lives entirely beneath the surface, invisible until the moment he chooses to unleash it.

Psychological Profile & Behavior

Out of Combat

Calm and observant. Sky enters every new space like a hunter assessing terrain — still, watching, cataloguing. He is not unfriendly, but he is unhurried. He speaks only when he has something worth saying. He waits before acting. Always.

In Combat

The patience carries over. Sky does not rush in. He watches, repositions, and waits for the precise moment — then strikes with absolute commitment. His Lawful Neutral code does not bend in a fight: he finishes what he starts.

Despite his fractured identity, Sky's loyalty to his master is his most consistent trait. It is not warm or expressive — it simply is. Like gravity. Unconditional and architectural.

Combat Mechanics & Flavor

All abilities manifest without incantation. Sky's void power is purely instinctive — expressed through motion, stance, and silence.

Void Talon Strike

Sky's primary attack. His talons tear through the air trailing wisps of shadow energy that vanish the instant contact ends, leaving no trace of what just tore through the target. Deals physical and void damage simultaneously.

Phantom Strike

Sky's shadow briefly fractures into two or three afterimages that strike from impossible angles. Enemies cannot tell which blow is real until they feel it. Mechanically similar to a multi-attack with disadvantage imposed on the defender.

Shadow Step

Sky dissolves into a pocket of void and re-emerges elsewhere within short range — no sound, no visual trail. Used to reposition after a strike, close distance in a single action, or escape a locked engagement. His signature defensive utility.

Damage Reactions

Predator Surfaces (Heavy Damage)

When Sky takes significant damage, he does not panic or retreat. Instead something older wakes up. The calm evaporates. His strikes become faster and less precise — pure hunting instinct overriding his trained discipline. The void energy bleeds visibly for the first time, flickering at his talons and eyes. This is the only moment his true nature is exposed.

DM Notes

Sky's playstyle leans on physical third-person narration rather than spoken dialogue. Ability flavoring should describe what the character does and how the world reacts — not what he says. Example: "Sky goes still. His shadow stretches the wrong direction for a half second — then he's already behind them, talons drawn."

His hidden void nature is a narrative lever. Enemies who survive encounters with him will not know what they fought. Allies may not know either — until they do.
